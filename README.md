## Hardware and documented limitations

**Robot:** SunFounder PiDog V2  
**Controller:** Raspberry Pi 5

During development, the SH3001 IMU and RGB strip failed initialization. These limitations are documented rather than presented as successful functions. The work also explores alternative behaviors with usable robot capabilities. Results and observed limitations should be interpreted in the context of the relevant lab report.
